## How to Trade on DEX

### Prerequisites
- Web3 wallet (MetaMask, Trust Wallet, etc.)
- ETH for gas fees
- Tokens to swap for PYRAX

### Step-by-Step Guide

**1. Connect Your Wallet**
1. Go to Uniswap (app.uniswap.org)
2. Click "Connect Wallet"
3. Select your wallet type
4. Approve connection

**2. Select Tokens**
1. Click token selector (top)
2. Choose what you're swapping FROM (e.g., ETH)
3. Click token selector (bottom)
4. Paste PYRAX contract address or search "PYRAX"
5. **Verify the correct contract!**

**3. Enter Amount**
1. Enter amount to swap
2. Review output amount
3. Check price impact (should be low)
4. Check slippage settings

**4. Execute Swap**
1. Click "Swap"
2. Review transaction details
3. Confirm in your wallet
4. Wait for confirmation

### Important DEX Tips

:::warning Verify Contract Address
Always verify the PYRAX contract address from official sources before trading. Scammers create fake tokens with similar names.

**Official Contract**: *Will be published at TGE*
:::

- **Slippage**: Set 1-3% for normal conditions
- **Price Impact**: Below 1% is ideal, above 5% is risky
- **Gas Fees**: Check current gas prices, wait for lower fees if not urgent
- **Liquidity**: More liquidity = better prices

## How to Trade on CEX

### Prerequisites
- Exchange account
- Completed KYC (usually required)
- Deposited funds

### Step-by-Step Guide

**1. Deposit Funds**
1. Log into exchange
2. Go to Deposit
3. Deposit fiat or crypto
4. Wait for confirmation

**2. Navigate to PYRAX**
1. Go to Markets or Trading
2. Search for "PYRAX"
3. Select trading pair (e.g., PYRAX/USDT)

**3. Place Order**
1. Choose order type:
   - **Market**: Buy immediately at current price
   - **Limit**: Set your desired price
2. Enter amount
3. Review total cost
4. Confirm order

**4. Withdraw to Personal Wallet (Recommended)**
1. Go to Withdraw
2. Select PYRAX
3. Enter your wallet address
4. Confirm withdrawal
5. Tokens arrive in your wallet

## Trading Strategies

### For New Traders

**Dollar Cost Averaging (DCA)**
- Buy fixed amount regularly (weekly/monthly)
- Reduces timing risk
- Works in any market condition

**Hold (HODL)**
- Buy and hold long-term
- Ignore short-term volatility
- Stake for additional rewards

### For Experienced Traders

**Swing Trading**
- Buy dips, sell rallies
- Requires market analysis
- Higher risk, higher potential reward

**Arbitrage**
- Buy on exchange with lower price
- Sell on exchange with higher price
- Requires speed and capital
