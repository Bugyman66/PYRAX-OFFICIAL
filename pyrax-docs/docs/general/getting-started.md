## The PYRAX Vision

PYRAX represents the next evolution in cryptocurrency mining. Instead of wasting computational power on meaningless calculations, PYRAX miners contribute to real-world AI workloads while still earning block rewards.

Think of it this way: traditional cryptocurrency mining is like running on a treadmill — you're expending energy but not going anywhere useful. PYRAX mining is like running deliveries — you're still getting exercise (earning rewards), but you're also doing something productive (processing AI tasks).

## Key Features

### 🖥️ Dual-Stream Mining
PYRAX uses a unique two-stream approach:
- **Stream A**: Traditional KAWPOW GPU mining for block rewards
- **Stream B**: AI compute jobs that pay additional PYRAX rewards

### 🤖 AI-Powered Computing
Your GPU doesn't just mine — it processes real AI workloads through our Crucible platform. This creates genuine economic value beyond just securing the network.

### 🗳️ Community Governance
PYRAX token holders have a say in the network's future through the DAO (Decentralized Autonomous Organization). Vote on proposals, suggest improvements, and shape the ecosystem.

### 💰 Multiple Income Streams
Earn PYRAX through:
- Block mining rewards
- AI compute job payments
- Staking rewards
- Liquidity provision
