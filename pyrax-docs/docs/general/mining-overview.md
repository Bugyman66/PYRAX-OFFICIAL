# Mining Overview

Mining is the process of using your computer's GPU to secure the PYRAX network and earn rewards. This page provides a comprehensive overview of PYRAX mining.

## What is Mining?

Cryptocurrency mining serves two essential purposes:

1. **Network Security** — Miners validate transactions and prevent fraud
2. **Token Distribution** — New tokens are fairly distributed to participants

When you mine PYRAX, your GPU solves complex mathematical puzzles. Successfully solving a puzzle allows you to add a new block to the blockchain and earn the associated reward.

## PYRAX Mining Features

### Dual-Stream Mining
PYRAX offers unique dual-income mining:
- **Stream A**: Block rewards (traditional mining)
- **Stream B**: AI compute payments (Crucible)

Learn more: [Dual-Stream Mining](./dual-stream-mining)

### KAWPOW Algorithm
PYRAX uses the KAWPOW algorithm:
- GPU-optimized for fair participation
- ASIC-resistant to prevent centralization
- Memory-intensive for security

### Fair Distribution
- No pre-mine advantage
- Equal opportunity for all miners
- Consistent block times (~60 seconds)

## Mining Methods

### Pool Mining (Recommended)

Join other miners to combine computing power:
- Consistent, regular payouts
- Lower variance in earnings
- Suitable for all GPU sizes

**How it works:**
1. Pool members share hashpower
2. Pool finds blocks together
3. Rewards split based on contribution
4. Small fee (typically 1-2%)

### Solo Mining

Mine independently:
- Keep entire block reward
- No pool fees
- Requires significant hashpower
- High variance in earnings

**Recommendation**: Unless you have substantial hashpower, pool mining is more practical.

## Important Considerations

### Electricity Costs
Mining consumes power. Calculate if profitable:
```
Monthly Cost = GPU Wattage × 24 × 30 × Electricity Rate
Example: 200W × 24 × 30 × $0.10 = $14.40/month
```

### Hardware Wear
- GPUs designed for continuous operation
- Maintain proper cooling
- Expected lifespan: 3-5+ years with care

### Heat Generation
- Mining produces significant heat
- Ensure adequate ventilation
- Can offset home heating in winter

### Noise
- Cooling fans run at high speeds
- Consider placement and fan curves
- Quieter at lower power settings

## Mining vs. Buying

| Mining | Buying |
|--------|--------|
| Gradual accumulation | Immediate ownership |
| Requires hardware investment | Requires capital |
| Ongoing electricity cost | One-time purchase |
| Learn the technology | Simpler process |
| Support network security | No network contribution |

**Best approach**: Consider doing both based on your situation.
