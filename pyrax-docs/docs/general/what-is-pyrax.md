# What is PYRAX?

PYRAX is a next-generation cryptocurrency that combines traditional GPU mining with artificial intelligence computing. It's designed to make mining meaningful by channeling computational power toward real-world AI tasks instead of wasting it on arbitrary calculations.

## The Problem with Traditional Mining

Traditional cryptocurrency mining works like this: thousands of computers race to solve complex mathematical puzzles. The winner gets to add the next block to the blockchain and receives a reward. While this secures the network, the actual calculations serve no purpose beyond the mining itself.

**Consider this**: The energy used by Bitcoin mining each year could power a small country. All that computational power produces nothing but blockchain security — no scientific discoveries, no AI breakthroughs, no practical output.

## The PYRAX Solution

PYRAX changes this equation by introducing **Dual-Stream Mining**:

### Stream A: Block Mining
Like traditional cryptocurrencies, PYRAX uses GPU mining to secure the network. Miners compete to solve cryptographic puzzles and earn block rewards. This ensures:
- Network security through proof-of-work
- Decentralized block production
- Fair distribution of new tokens

### Stream B: AI Compute
Here's where PYRAX gets interesting. Your GPU can simultaneously process AI workloads through our **Crucible** platform:
- Run machine learning inference tasks
- Process AI model training jobs
- Execute computer vision algorithms
- Earn additional PYRAX for completed work

## How It Works (Simple Explanation)

Imagine you own a powerful computer. With traditional mining, it's like having your computer do endless sudoku puzzles — good for security, but not much else.

With PYRAX:
1. **Your GPU mines blocks** (earning block rewards)
2. **Your GPU also accepts AI jobs** (earning job payments)
3. **You earn from both activities** (double income potential)

The AI jobs come from businesses and researchers who need computing power but don't want to pay expensive cloud providers like AWS or Google Cloud. They pay in PYRAX, which goes directly to miners.

## Key Components

### The PYRAX Token
PYRAX is the native cryptocurrency of the network. It's used for:
- **Mining rewards** — Earned by block producers
- **Job payments** — Paid to GPU providers for AI work
- **Staking** — Lock tokens to earn passive rewards
- **Governance** — Vote on network proposals
- **Transaction fees** — Pay for network operations

### Crucible Platform
Crucible is PYRAX's AI compute marketplace. It connects:
- **Job Submitters** — People who need AI computing power
- **GPU Providers** — Miners who offer their GPUs for AI work
- **Verification Nodes** — Ensure job results are accurate

### The Foundry
The Foundry is PYRAX's mining hub. It manages:
- Pool mining coordination
- Job distribution to GPUs
- Reward calculations and distribution
- Network difficulty adjustments

## Why PYRAX Matters
