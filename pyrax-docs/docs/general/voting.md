# Voting Guide

This guide explains how to vote on PYRAX DAO proposals and make your voice heard in network governance.

## Voting Basics

### Who Can Vote?
Anyone holding PYRAX tokens can vote. More tokens = more voting power.

### Voting Power Calculation

| Factor | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| PYRAX held | Base voting power |
| Staking duration | Multiplier bonus |
| Delegation received | Added to your power |

**Example:**
- 5,000 PYRAX unstaked = 5,000 votes
- 5,000 PYRAX in 90-day stake (1.25x) = 6,250 votes

## How to Vote

### Step 1: Access the DAO Portal
1. Visit [dao.pyrax.org](https://dao.pyrax.org)
2. Click "Connect Wallet"
3. Select your wallet (MetaMask, etc.)
4. Approve the connection

### Step 2: Find Active Proposals
1. Click "Proposals" tab
2. Filter by "Active" status
3. Browse current proposals

### Step 3: Review Proposal
Before voting:
- Read the full proposal text
- Check discussion comments
- Understand implications
- Review author's history

### Step 4: Cast Your Vote
1. Click on the proposal
2. Select your choice:
   - **For** — Support the proposal
   - **Against** — Oppose the proposal
   - **Abstain** — Neutral (counts toward quorum)
3. Click "Submit Vote"
4. Confirm transaction in wallet
5. Pay small gas fee

### Step 5: Verify Your Vote
- Vote appears in proposal's vote list
- Transaction visible on block explorer
- Your voting power recorded

## Vote Options Explained

### For
You support the proposal passing. Use when:
- You agree with the change
- Benefits outweigh risks
- Aligns with your vision for PYRAX

### Against
You oppose the proposal. Use when:
- You disagree with the change
- Risks outweigh benefits
- Better alternatives exist

### Abstain
Neutral position. Use when:
- You're uncertain
- Want to help reach quorum
- Don't want to influence outcome

## Understanding Quorum

Quorum is the minimum participation required for a valid vote.

| Proposal Type | Quorum Required |
|---------------|-----------------|
| Standard | 4% of supply |
| Constitutional | 10% of supply |
| Emergency | 5% of supply |

**If quorum isn't reached**, the proposal fails regardless of vote ratio.

## Voting Strategies

### Active Participation
- Vote on every proposal
- Engage in discussions
- Build governance reputation

### Selective Voting
- Focus on impactful proposals
- Skip minor parameter changes
- Prioritize your expertise areas

### Delegation
- Delegate to trusted community members
- Good if you lack time to research
- Can reclaim power anytime

## Delegation

### What is Delegation?
Transfer your voting power to someone else while keeping your tokens.

### How to Delegate
1. Go to DAO dashboard
2. Click "Delegate"
3. Enter delegate's address
4. Confirm transaction
5. Delegate now votes for you

### Choosing a Delegate
Look for delegates who:
- Vote consistently
- Align with your values
- Are active in governance
- Have transparent track records

### Reclaiming Votes
1. Return to Delegate section
2. Click "Undelegate" or "Delegate to Self"
3. Confirm transaction
4. Voting power returns to you

## Voting Tips

### Do Your Research
- Read the full proposal
- Check Discord/forum discussions
- Understand technical implications
- Consider long-term effects

### Consider Trade-offs
Every proposal has pros and cons:
- Short-term vs long-term
- User vs developer impact
- Security vs convenience

### Vote Your Values
- What kind of network do you want?
- Who benefits from this change?
- Does it align with PYRAX mission?

### Don't Panic Vote
- Take time to understand
- Voting period is 7 days
- Better to be informed than fast

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I change my vote?
No, votes are final once submitted.

### Do I need to unstake to vote?
No, staked tokens can vote (with bonus multiplier).

### What if I buy more PYRAX during voting?
Voting power is snapshot at proposal creation. New tokens don't affect active proposals.

### Can I vote from multiple wallets?
Yes, each wallet votes with its own tokens.

### What happens if I delegate then buy more PYRAX?
New tokens aren't automatically delegated. Delegate again or vote directly.
