### How to Buy in Presale

**Step 1: Prepare Your Wallet**
- Use MetaMask, Trust Wallet, or similar
- Ensure you have ETH/USDT/USDC for purchase
- Have some ETH for gas fees

**Step 2: Visit the Presale Page**
1. Go to [pyrax.org](https://pyrax.org)
2. Click "Join Presale" button
3. Connect your wallet

**Step 3: Make Your Purchase**
1. Select payment currency (ETH, USDT, USDC)
2. Enter amount to spend
3. See how many PYRAX you'll receive
4. Click "Buy Now"
5. Confirm in your wallet

**Step 4: Claim Tokens**
- Tokens are distributed at Token Generation Event (TGE)
- Connect same wallet to claim
- Follow announcements for claim date

### Presale Benefits

## Cryptocurrency Exchanges

After launch, PYRAX will be tradable on exchanges.

### Planned Listings

**Decentralized Exchanges (DEXs)**
- Uniswap
- SushiSwap
- PancakeSwap (if BSC bridge available)

**Centralized Exchanges (CEXs)**
- Announcements coming after TGE
- Multiple tier-1 and tier-2 exchanges targeted

### How to Buy on DEX

**Step 1: Setup**
1. Install MetaMask or similar wallet
2. Add PYRAX network (if native) or Ethereum
3. Fund wallet with ETH for gas + purchase

**Step 2: Connect to DEX**
1. Go to Uniswap (or other DEX)
2. Connect wallet
3. Find PYRAX token (verify contract address!)

**Step 3: Swap**
1. Select token to swap from (ETH, USDC, etc.)
2. Select PYRAX as output
3. Enter amount
4. Review price impact and fees
5. Confirm swap

### How to Buy on CEX

**Step 1: Create Account**
1. Sign up on exchange
2. Complete KYC verification
3. Enable 2FA security

**Step 2: Deposit Funds**
1. Deposit fiat (USD, EUR, etc.) or crypto
2. Wait for confirmation

**Step 3: Buy PYRAX**
1. Navigate to PYRAX trading pair
2. Choose order type (market or limit)
3. Enter amount
4. Confirm purchase

**Step 4: Withdraw (Recommended)**
1. Go to withdrawal page
2. Enter your PYRAX wallet address
3. Confirm withdrawal
4. Tokens arrive in your personal wallet
