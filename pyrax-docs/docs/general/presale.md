## How to Participate

### Step 1: Prepare Your Wallet

You'll need a Web3 wallet:
- **MetaMask** (recommended for desktop)
- **Trust Wallet** (recommended for mobile)
- **Coinbase Wallet**
- **WalletConnect** compatible wallets

### Step 2: Fund Your Wallet

Add funds to your wallet:
- **ETH** — For gas fees and purchase
- **USDT** — Stablecoin option
- **USDC** — Stablecoin option
- **BNB** — If using BNB Chain

**Tip**: Always have extra ETH for gas fees (~$10-20)

### Step 3: Connect to Presale

1. Visit [pyrax.org](https://pyrax.org)
2. Click "Join Presale" or "Buy Now"
3. Click "Connect Wallet"
4. Select your wallet type
5. Approve the connection

### Step 4: Make Your Purchase

1. Select payment currency (ETH, USDT, USDC)
2. Enter amount you want to spend
3. Review PYRAX tokens you'll receive
4. Review bonus tokens included
5. Click "Buy Now"
6. Confirm transaction in wallet
7. Wait for confirmation

### Step 5: Track Your Purchase

After purchase:
- Transaction appears in your wallet history
- Tokens tracked in presale dashboard
- Connect wallet to see your allocation

## Security Checklist

Before participating, verify:

✅ **Correct URL**: pyrax.org (bookmark it)
✅ **Secure connection**: HTTPS padlock visible
✅ **Official links**: From verified social media
✅ **Contract verification**: Matches official announcements
✅ **No seed phrase requests**: Never share your seed phrase

:::danger Common Scams
- Fake presale websites with similar URLs
- Direct messages offering "exclusive" access
- Requests for seed phrases or private keys
- Promises of guaranteed returns
- Unofficial Telegram/Discord admins

**If in doubt, ask in official Discord before proceeding!**
:::

Yes, you can make multiple purchases. All allocations are tracked to your wallet.
