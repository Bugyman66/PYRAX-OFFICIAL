### Halving Events

Unlike Bitcoin's abrupt halvings, PYRAX uses a smooth emission curve that decreases by approximately 20% annually. This provides:
- **Predictability** — Miners can plan for gradual changes
- **Stability** — No sudden supply shocks
- **Sustainability** — Mining remains viable long-term

## Token Utility

PYRAX serves multiple purposes within the ecosystem:

### 1. Transaction Fees
Every transaction on the PYRAX network requires a small fee paid in PYRAX. This:
- Prevents spam attacks
- Compensates validators
- Creates consistent token demand

### 2. Mining Rewards
Miners who successfully produce blocks receive PYRAX rewards. This:
- Secures the network
- Distributes tokens fairly
- Incentivizes participation

### 3. AI Compute Payments
Users pay PYRAX to GPU providers for processing AI workloads:
- Job submitters pay in PYRAX
- GPU providers earn PYRAX
- Creates real economic utility

### 4. Staking
Token holders can stake PYRAX to:
- Earn passive income (5-15% APY)
- Participate in governance
- Support network security

### 5. Governance
PYRAX holders vote on:
- Protocol upgrades
- Treasury spending
- Fee parameters
- Ecosystem grants

## Economic Mechanisms

### Burn Mechanisms
PYRAX implements strategic token burns to create deflationary pressure:

| Burn Source | Rate | Effect |
|-------------|------|--------|
| Transaction fees | 50% burned | Reduces supply over time |
| AI job fees | 10% burned | Links AI growth to scarcity |
| Penalty burns | 100% burned | Punishes bad actors |

### Staking Rewards
Staking rewards come from:
- 50% of transaction fees
- Portion of AI compute fees
- Treasury allocations

The staking APY adjusts based on:
- Total amount staked (higher stake = lower APY)
- Network activity (more activity = higher rewards)
- Governance decisions

## Vesting Schedules

### Team & Development (10%)
To align team incentives with long-term success:
- **12-month cliff** — No tokens released for first year
- **36-month vesting** — Monthly releases after cliff
- **Total duration** — 48 months to full unlock

### BDAG Community (10%)
For BlockDAG token holders migrating to PYRAX:
- **6-month cliff** — Tokens locked initially
- **12-month vesting** — Monthly releases
- **Total duration** — 18 months to full unlock

### Treasury (5%)
Controlled by DAO governance:
- Released based on approved proposals
- Minimum 7-day voting period
- Requires majority approval

## Why This Design?

### Large Supply (100 Billion)
- Makes individual tokens affordable
- Psychological appeal (owning "whole" tokens)
- Room for micro-transactions

### 40% to Mining
- Ensures long-term miner participation
- Distributes tokens fairly
- Decentralizes ownership over time

### Multiple Burn Mechanisms
- Creates sustainable deflation
- Ties value to actual usage
- Rewards long-term holders

### Vesting for Insiders
- Prevents dump and abandonment
- Aligns team with community
- Builds trust through commitment
