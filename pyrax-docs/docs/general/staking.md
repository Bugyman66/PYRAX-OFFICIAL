# Staking PYRAX

Staking allows you to earn passive income by locking your PYRAX tokens. This guide explains how staking works and how to get started.

## What is Staking?

Staking is like putting your tokens in a savings account. You commit to holding them for a period, and in return, you earn rewards.

### Why Stake?

| Benefit | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Passive income** | Earn PYRAX without active work |
| **Support the network** | Staked tokens strengthen ecosystem |
| **Governance power** | Stakers can vote on proposals |
| **Compound growth** | Reinvest rewards for exponential gains |

## How PYRAX Staking Works

### Staking Pools

PYRAX offers different staking options:

| Pool | Lock Period | Base APY | Bonus |
|------|-------------|----------|-------|
| **Flexible** | None | 3-5% | None |
| **30-Day** | 30 days | 6-8% | +1% governance bonus |
| **90-Day** | 90 days | 9-12% | +2% governance bonus |
| **365-Day** | 1 year | 12-15% | +5% governance bonus |

*APY rates are approximate and adjust based on total staked*

### Reward Sources

Staking rewards come from:
- **Transaction fees** — 50% of network fees go to stakers
- **AI compute fees** — Portion of Crucible revenue
- **Inflation rewards** — Small allocation from emission

### Dynamic APY

The actual APY adjusts based on:
- **Total staked** — More stakers = lower individual APY
- **Network activity** — More transactions = higher rewards
- **Lock duration** — Longer = better rate

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Get PYRAX Tokens

You need PYRAX to stake. Acquire tokens through:
- [Presale](./presale)
- [Mining](./mining-overview)
- [Exchanges](./exchanges)

### Step 2: Choose Your Platform

**PYRAX Desktop (Recommended)**
1. Download from [pyrax.org/downloads](https://pyrax.org/downloads)
2. Create or import wallet
3. Navigate to Staking tab

**Web Interface**
1. Visit [stake.pyrax.org](https://stake.pyrax.org)
2. Connect your wallet
3. Access staking dashboard

### Step 3: Select a Pool

Consider your needs:
- **Need flexibility?** → Flexible pool
- **Want best returns?** → 365-Day pool
- **Balanced approach?** → 90-Day pool

### Step 4: Stake Your Tokens

1. Enter amount to stake
2. Confirm the lock period
3. Approve the transaction
4. Sign with your wallet

### Step 5: Earn & Manage

- Watch rewards accumulate
- Claim rewards anytime (or auto-compound)
- Unstake when lock period ends

## Staking Strategies

### Conservative Strategy
**Goal**: Steady income with flexibility

- 70% in Flexible pool
- 30% in 30-Day pool
- Claim and sell rewards regularly

**Best for**: Those who may need funds

### Balanced Strategy
**Goal**: Good returns with moderate commitment

- 50% in 90-Day pool
- 30% in 30-Day pool
- 20% in Flexible pool

**Best for**: Most users

### Aggressive Strategy
**Goal**: Maximum returns

- 80% in 365-Day pool
- 20% in 90-Day pool
- Auto-compound all rewards

**Best for**: Long-term believers

### DCA Staking
**Goal**: Build position over time

- Stake a fixed amount weekly/monthly
- Spread across different pools
- Reduces timing risk

**Best for**: Regular income earners

## Understanding Rewards

### Calculation Example

**Scenario**: 
- Stake: 100,000 PYRAX
- Pool: 90-Day
- APY: 10%

**Monthly rewards**:
```
100,000 × 10% ÷ 12 = 833 PYRAX/month
```

**After 1 year (compounding monthly)**:
```
100,000 × (1 + 0.10/12)^12 = 110,471 PYRAX
```

### Claiming vs. Compounding

| Strategy | Approach | Best When |
|----------|----------|-----------|
| **Claim** | Take rewards as earned | Need income now |
| **Compound** | Restake rewards | Long-term growth |
| **Hybrid** | Claim some, compound some | Balanced needs |

## Risks & Considerations

### Lock Period Risk
- Tokens locked until period ends
- Can't sell during market changes
- Emergency unstake has penalty

### Smart Contract Risk
- Staking involves smart contracts
- Contracts are audited but not risk-free
- Only stake what you can afford to lock

### Opportunity Cost
- Locked tokens can't be used elsewhere
- Missing other investment opportunities
- Consider your full portfolio

### APY Fluctuation
- Rates change with participation
- Not guaranteed returns
- Market conditions affect real value

## Emergency Unstaking

If you need tokens before lock ends:

| Pool | Early Unstake Penalty |
|------|----------------------|
| Flexible | None |
| 30-Day | 5% of staked amount |
| 90-Day | 10% of staked amount |
| 365-Day | 20% of staked amount |

**Process:**
1. Go to Staking dashboard
2. Click "Emergency Unstake"
3. Acknowledge penalty
4. Confirm transaction
5. Receive tokens minus penalty

:::warning
Emergency unstaking forfeits accumulated rewards and incurs the penalty. Only use in genuine emergencies.
:::

## FAQ

### When do I receive rewards?
Rewards accumulate continuously. Claim anytime or enable auto-compound.

### Can I add to an existing stake?
Yes, you can add tokens to flexible and some locked pools. Adding extends some lock periods.

### What happens when lock ends?
Tokens become claimable. They don't auto-unstake — you must claim them.

### Are rewards taxable?
Depends on your jurisdiction. Consult a tax professional. Staking rewards are typically taxable income.

### What's the minimum stake?
No minimum for flexible pool. Locked pools may have minimums (typically 100-1000 PYRAX).
