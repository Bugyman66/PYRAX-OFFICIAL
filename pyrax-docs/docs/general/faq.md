# Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about PYRAX.

## General Questions

### What is PYRAX?
PYRAX is a next-generation cryptocurrency that combines traditional GPU mining with AI computing. Miners earn from both block rewards and processing AI workloads through our Dual-Stream Mining system.

### What makes PYRAX different from other cryptocurrencies?
PYRAX is unique because your mining power does useful work. Instead of only solving arbitrary puzzles, your GPU also processes real AI jobs through our Crucible platform, earning you additional income while contributing to meaningful computation.

### Is PYRAX a token or a coin?
PYRAX is a native coin on its own blockchain, not a token on another network. It uses the KAWPOW proof-of-work algorithm optimized for GPU mining.

### What is the total supply of PYRAX?
The maximum supply is 100 billion PYRAX tokens, released gradually through mining emissions over many years.

You can acquire PYRAX through:

## Mining Questions

### What hardware do I need to mine PYRAX?
You need a GPU with at least 6GB VRAM. NVIDIA GTX 1060 6GB or AMD RX 580 8GB are the minimum. Better GPUs earn more.

### Can I mine with a laptop?
Technically yes, but not recommended. Laptops have limited cooling and may overheat. Desktop GPUs are much more suitable.

### How much can I earn mining?
Earnings depend on your GPU, electricity costs, network difficulty, and PYRAX price. Check our mining calculator for estimates based on your hardware.

### What is Dual-Stream Mining?
Dual-Stream Mining allows your GPU to earn from two sources simultaneously:
- **Stream A**: Block rewards (traditional mining)
- **Stream B**: AI compute job payments (Crucible)

### Do I have to use both streams?
No, Stream B (AI compute) is optional. You can mine traditionally with Stream A only, but you'd miss additional earnings.

### Can I mine PYRAX on multiple computers?
Yes, you can mine with as many GPUs as you want. Each connects to the pool with your wallet address.

## Wallet Questions

### What wallet should I use for PYRAX?
We recommend:
- **PYRAX Desktop** — Official wallet with built-in mining
- **MetaMask** — Popular Web3 wallet (add PYRAX network)
- **Hardware wallets** — Ledger/Trezor for large holdings

### How do I backup my wallet?
Your wallet is backed up by saving your seed phrase (12 or 24 words). Write it on paper and store securely. Never store digitally.

### What if I lose my seed phrase?
Without your seed phrase, there's no way to recover your wallet. This is why secure backup is critical.

### Can I use the same wallet for mining and trading?
Yes, one wallet address works for all PYRAX activities.

## Staking Questions

### What is staking?
Staking means locking your PYRAX tokens to earn passive rewards. Longer lock periods earn higher APY.

### What's the staking APY?
APY varies by lock period:
- Flexible: 3-5%
- 30-day: 6-8%
- 90-day: 9-12%
- 365-day: 12-15%

Rates adjust based on total staked and network activity.

### Can I unstake early?
Yes, but with a penalty. Emergency unstaking forfeits rewards and incurs 5-20% penalty depending on lock period.

### How do I stake my PYRAX?
Through PYRAX Desktop or the staking portal at [stake.pyrax.org](https://stake.pyrax.org). Connect wallet, choose pool, stake tokens.

## DAO & Governance

### What is the PYRAX DAO?
The DAO is the decentralized governance system where PYRAX holders vote on protocol decisions, treasury spending, and network upgrades.

### How do I vote?
1. Hold PYRAX tokens (staked for bonus voting power)
2. Visit [dao.pyrax.org](https://dao.pyrax.org)
3. Connect wallet
4. Vote on active proposals

### How much voting power do I have?
1 PYRAX = 1 base vote. Staked tokens receive up to 1.5x multiplier for long-term stakes.

### Can I create proposals?
Yes, if you hold at least 100,000 PYRAX (or have it delegated to you).

## Crucible & AI Compute

### What is Crucible?
Crucible is PYRAX's decentralized AI computing marketplace. It connects people who need GPU computing power with miners willing to process AI jobs.

### What kind of AI jobs can I process?
- Machine learning inference
- Image/video processing
- Model training
- Natural language processing
- Scientific computing

### How do I earn from Crucible?
Enable Stream B in PYRAX Desktop. Your GPU will receive AI jobs and you'll earn PYRAX for completed work.

### Is my data safe when processing AI jobs?
Jobs run in isolated containers. Your GPU processes data but doesn't store it. Data is deleted after completion.

## Security Questions

### Is PYRAX safe?
The PYRAX protocol is designed with security in mind. However, your personal security depends on following best practices like protecting your seed phrase and avoiding scams.

### How do I protect my PYRAX?
- Never share your seed phrase
- Use hardware wallets for large amounts
- Enable 2FA on all accounts
- Verify URLs before connecting wallet
- Be skeptical of unsolicited DMs

### What if I'm scammed?
Immediately move remaining funds to a new wallet. Document everything and report to community moderators. Unfortunately, crypto transactions are irreversible.

### Will PYRAX team ever DM me?
No. Official team members will NEVER DM you first. Anyone claiming to be support in DMs is a scammer.
